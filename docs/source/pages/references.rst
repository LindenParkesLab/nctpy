References
==============================

When using nctpy, please cite the protocol paper:

* `Parkes, L., Kim, J. Z., Stiso, J., Brynildsen, J. K., Cieslak, M., Covitz, S., Gur, R. E., Gur, R. C., Pasqualetti, F., Shinohara, R. T., Zhou, D., Satterthwaite, T. D., & Bassett, D. S. (2024). A network control theory pipeline for studying the dynamics of the structural connectome. Nature Protocols, 19, 3721–3749. <https://doi.org/10.1038/s41596-024-01023-w>`_

When fitting decay rates with ``nctpy.optimize``, please also cite:

* `Kim, J. Z., Betzel, R. F., Beyh, A., Howell, A., Kuceyeski, A., Larsen, B., Seguin, C., Zhang, X.-H., Holmes, A., & Parkes, L. (2025). Inferring intrinsic neural timescales using optimal control theory. Nature Communications, 16, 11639. <https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-66542-w>`_

Introduction to relevant linear systems theory

* `Kim, J. Z., & Bassett, D. S. Linear dynamics & control of brain networks. arXiv (2019). <https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.03309>`_

First publication applying control metrics used in this package to human structural brain data

First publication applying optimal control energy used in this package to human structural brain data

* `Gu, S., Betzel R. F., Mattar, M. G. et al. Optimal trajectories of brain state transitions. NeuroImage (2017). <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroimage.2017.01.003>`_
